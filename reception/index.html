Talk through your setup
Will TheAiAutomators replace our reception team?+

TheAiAutomators is designed to take routine inquiries off your team’s hands. Your staff stay responsible for sensitive conversations, exceptions and requests that need a person.

Can it book directly into our calendar?+

Direct booking depends on your calendar or booking system and the connection available. Where direct booking is unavailable, TheAiAutomators can capture appointment preferences for staff confirmation.

Can we keep our existing phone number?+

We assess forwarding or connection options with your current phone provider during setup. A change of number is not assumed.

What happens when a caller wants a person?+

We agree on a handoff or callback process with your team. A live transfer requires a supported phone connection and an available staff member.

Which languages can we use?+

English and Hindi are the first languages we plan to evaluate for Indian businesses. We confirm voice quality and language support for your use case before including them in a proposal.

When can I try the voice demo?+

The voice demo is coming soon. Contact us to discuss a setup for your business.
