BEFORE YOU DECIDE

The questions
worth asking.

What does the starting price include?+

The ₹35,000 Standard Website includes 5–7 pages, professional design, mobile responsiveness, business-specific copy, contact actions, forms, Google Maps, booking enquiries, basic SEO, analytics, hosting and domain setup, SSL and launch. Domain registration and paid third-party tools are itemised in your quote.

Can you redesign our existing website?+

Yes. We review what is useful, keep the information your visitors need and rebuild the path to an enquiry. Existing domain, content and booking connections are reviewed before work starts.

Do we need to write all the content?+

We write the website copy with information from your team. You supply your business details, services and approved photos, then review the wording before launch. Clinic information and medical claims require your approval.

How long will it take?+

A typical agreed build targets 2–4 weeks from kickoff. Content, feedback and booking connections affect the timeline. Your proposal sets the actual schedule.

What happens after launch?+

You receive a handover and the access agreed in your proposal. Standard care is ₹2,000/month for hosting, SSL, backups, monitoring, minor content updates and technical maintenance. Custom care is ₹3,000–₹5,000/month. Your proposal defines update allowances and any third-party costs.

Will the website guarantee more bookings?+

No website can promise a sales result. We improve the things visitors need to make a decision: clear services, credible information and a simple enquiry path. Real enquiries and your team’s follow-up are the measures that matter.
