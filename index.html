Will TheAiAutomators replace our reception team?+

TheAiAutomators is designed to take routine inquiries off your team’s hands. Your staff stay responsible for sensitive conversations, exceptions and requests that need a person.

Can it book directly into our calendar?+

Direct booking depends on your calendar or booking system and the connection available. Where direct booking is unavailable, TheAiAutomators can capture appointment preferences for staff confirmation.

Can we keep our existing phone number?+

We assess forwarding or connection options with your current phone provider during setup. A change of number is not assumed.

What happens when a caller wants a person?+

We agree on a handoff or callback process with your team. A live transfer requires a supported phone connection and an available staff member.

Which languages can we use?+

English and Hindi are the first languages we plan to evaluate for Indian businesses. We confirm voice quality and language support for your use case before including them in a proposal.

TheAiAutomators, EXPLAINED

What is TheAiAutomators?

TheAiAutomators provides AI receptionist and website services for clinics and hotels in India. TheAiAutomators Reception is designed to answer approved routine questions, capture booking requests and follow an agreed staff handoff. TheAiAutomators Sites builds the website; TheAiAutomators Front Desk brings the two together. Reception plans start at ₹7,999 per month for Starter and ₹14,999 per month for Growth, with custom pricing for multi-location clinics. Website projects are priced separately from ₹35,000.

A live setup’s booking connections, language support, operating hours and launch timing are agreed for each business. The voice demo is coming soon.

What is an AI receptionist?

An AI receptionist is software that uses spoken conversation to handle routine telephone inquiries. Depending on its configuration, it can share approved information, collect a booking request and transfer or prepare a callback for staff. A booking is confirmed only through the authorised booking process.
