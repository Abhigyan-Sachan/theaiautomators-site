Can TheAiAutomators handle calls for more than one Mumbai branch?+

Location-specific hours, services and routing can be included in a proposal. We first review how callers choose a branch and which team should receive each inquiry.

Which languages can we use?+

English and Hindi are the first languages planned for evaluation. We test the selected voice system with your scenarios before confirming either in a proposal. Marathi support is assessed separately; it is not assumed.

How would TheAiAutomators fit our Mumbai front desk?+

We review your hours, services, phone connection, booking process and staff handoff. Direct confirmation and live transfers depend on supported connections. Timing and service commitments are agreed in your proposal.

How much does TheAiAutomators cost?+
